But what is dy/dx

Episode 1 · Calculus · Arabic · ~1 hour

The gap between knowing the derivative formulas and understanding what a derivative actually means, and why AI needs it. Watch it before a calculus or ML/DL course and the rest of the course feels a lot more comfortable.

Watch on YouTube Interactive notebook Channel @gedawy292

What the episode covers

  1. What dy/dx means: slope, limits, and why the formulas work
  2. Multivariate functions and partial derivatives
  3. The chain rule, built up through several examples
  4. Fitting a line to points with gradient descent
  5. Backpropagation as the chain rule, applied intuitively

Why I started

I've explained concepts for years, always to a room: small groups and large ones, kids and adults, onsite and online, with slides and live interaction. Videos are the new format. I posted the idea on Twitter first, and enough people liked it that I started with calculus:
